## Overview

This assignment explores examples of artificial intelligence in popular culture and considers the language-processing abilities these systems would need.

## Part 1 – Pop Culture Examples

- **Holly (Red Dwarf):** The spaceship's AI communicates with the crew, understands questions and instructions, provides information, and helps manage the ship.
- **GLaDOS (Portal):** An AI that manages a testing facility, provides spoken instructions, communicates with the player, and reacts to actions.

## Part 2 – Behind the Scenes: Holly

I selected Holly to explain three language-related abilities:

1. **Speech recognition:** Recognizing spoken words from crew members.
2. **Understanding meaning and context:** Interpreting questions, commands, and the situation.
3. **Responding and helping:** Giving appropriate spoken responses, providing information, and controlling the spaceship when requested.
